Founder & CEO
Aryan Mehta
Product Designer turned Full-Stack Maker

Aryan spent four years building marketing assets for early-stage SaaS companies, and every single time, he found himself wrestling with Photoshop for hours just to produce a simple, convincing WhatsApp screenshot. The process was slow, painful, and utterly unnecessary — so he decided to build the tool he always wished existed.

MockDrop was born in a single weekend hackathon and shipped to Product Hunt six weeks later. What started as a personal itch has since grown into the go-to mockup engine for over 2,000 creators, marketers, and founders across 40+ countries. Aryan believes that great marketing shouldn't require a design degree — that AI should handle the busywork so teams can focus on telling better stories. He's deeply committed to keeping MockDrop honest: no dark patterns, no hidden fees, and no watermarks on the free plan, ever. When he's not shipping features, you'll find him experimenting with generative design, hiking somewhere with no WiFi, or mentoring fellow indie hackers on their first Product Hunt launch.
