From your problem statement to your financial model — MockDrop flags what VCs notice in the first 60 seconds.

01
Problem Slide
Is the pain real, urgent, and underserved — or is this a vitamin, not a painkiller?
02
Market Opportunity
TAM/SAM/SOM — is the sizing bottom-up or just "1% of a trillion dollar market"?
03
Solution & Product
Does the solution actually match the problem, and is there a demo or prototype?
04
Business Model
How do you make money? What's the unit economics story — CAC, LTV, payback?
05
Competitive Landscape

Team Slide
Why does this team have the right to build this — background, domain, conviction?
08
Ask & Use of Funds
Is the raise size calibrated to milestones — or just a round number with no logic?

Founder & CEO
Amrita Maurya
Product Designer turned Full-Stack Maker

Amrita spent four years watching founders pitch the same avoidable mistakes over and over — vague market sizing pulled from thin air, moats that weren't defensible, team slides that buried the strongest credential on slide 14. She built MockDrop after her own first startup got torn apart in a partner meeting for reasons she could have fixed if someone had just told her beforehand.

MockDrop was born in a single weekend and shipped six weeks later. What started as a tool for founders in Amrita's network has grown into the go-to pitch preparation platform for 2,000+ founders across 40+ countries. She believes that fundraising is a skill, not a lottery — and that honest, specific feedback, delivered without ego, can fundamentally change outcomes for companies that deserve to exist. When she's not shipping features, you'll find her judging pitch competitions, mentoring pre-seed founders, or attempting to summit something without cell service.
